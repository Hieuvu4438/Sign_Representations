The current feature producer is Linear(LayerNorm(latent)). Its ideal normalized
latent has zero mean and squared norm<=d. For gallery g, a=W^Tg, v=gamma*a,
the scalar feature score lies in beta·a ± sqrt(d)*||v-mean(v)||. Multiplication by
unknown clamped activity in[0,1] includes zero in that interval. Sum observed
contributions plus all unobserved envelopes; stop only if a class lower bound
exceeds every other upper bound and observed weight sum>1e-12. Otherwise encode
all windows. This preserves the native all-zero-activity fallback branch.

These are ideal-arithmetic bounds, **not an audited floating-point certificate**.
The diagnostic checks saved finite FP32 features against the envelope and dense
FP64 pooling/ranking. Empirical agreement cannot establish universal GPU bounds.
Top1 certification alone cannot promise unchanged full ranks/DCG. Cached simulated
window counts are not measured encoder or end-to-end savings. Stop immediately
on provenance, envelope or reference mismatch; preserve failed runs. No widening
after outcomes. If bounds are vacuous, kill this deterministic recipe's GPU scale;
do not fit a maximum on these outcomes and call it a global bound.

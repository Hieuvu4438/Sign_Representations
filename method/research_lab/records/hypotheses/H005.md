# H005 — Participant geometry and cross-participant dictionary lookup

Registered diagnostic only, 2026-10-04; owner Codex; direction `frozen_probe`.
Contribution candidate: analysis; no method novelty or GPU promotion. D023.

Question: do fixed SignRep full-activity clip vectors encode a reproducible
participant-associated signal across disjoint gloss sets? If yes, does that
signal actually impair signer-separated dictionary retrieval? The second
question is **not** answered by the first diagnostic.

Use case: few-example ASL video-to-dictionary lookup for a query participant
absent from the training gallery. Existing P001 metadata supports a bounded
cheap probe before inventing a new objective. COHORT001 train has1313 sources,
32 participant IDs,1242 class×participant cells (71 repeated cells); val has378
sources/five IDs. The diagnostic uses only train embeddings and official
participant metadata, not manual linguistic/crop annotations.

H005_v0 predicts cross-gloss participant centroid balanced accuracy will exceed
the median of100 within-gloss shuffled-label controls by at least0.15 (15pp),
with >=.95 candidate coverage in both fixed directions. This is a prospective
screening threshold for investing in a task-harm test, not a literature-derived
practical retrieval gain or hypothesis significance level. Alternative: weak
participant signal, label/gloss frequency effects, camera/background/session
association, rare-ID coverage, legitimate signing variation. Participant IDs
are not a guarantee of independently verified personal identity.

Mechanism under investigation: participant-associated geometry could influence
support similarity. No representation is changed, no information is removed,
no causal nuisance designation and no retrieval gain predicted yet. Appearance,
timing and form may carry valid linguistic signal. Do not remove all signer
variation based on an ID probe.

Cheapest falsifier: H005_v0, one SHA-ordered50/50-gloss partition, both directions,
nearest normalized participant mean, all1313 train sources evaluated once;
100 controls with exact within-gloss label multisets preserved. Primary mean
of the two fold balanced recalls; secondary micro, coverage, per-ID denominators
and train-majority control. Zero HPO, seed1701,180s CPU,0GPU,<32MiB arrays and
<5MiB durable records. All failed trials kept; no threshold/seed sweep.

Closest works: S03/S13 native frozen dictionary retrieval; S30 signer-adversarial
learning (institutional abstract only); S31 few-shot hubness (proceedings abstract
only). Predictability is not hubness, adversarial signer invariance is prior art,
and changing encoder/task alone is not new. Closest full texts/code/novelty
unresolved: pilot baseline failure is allowed, scaling/new-method claims held.

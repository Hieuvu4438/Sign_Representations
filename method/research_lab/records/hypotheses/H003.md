- Status: hold (draft, not registered); owner Codex; 2026-10-03.

- Contribution candidate: analysis → method only if mechanism measured.
- Sources S01,L01,S03; protocol not created, inputs/masks unknown.

Question: reliability-aware adaptation có giảm degradation dưới natural tracking
failure hơn zero fill, dropout và imputation mà giữ clean recognition quality?
Use case motivated by tracking/interpolation S01; chưa có local failure rates.
Hypothesis: explicit observed-confidence/missingness có giá trị hơn inferred
zeros. Không mặc định face/hand variation là nuisance; erased linguistic cues
có thể làm nhãn không còn inferable.

Primary proposed: corrupted-versus-clean micro Top1 degradation trên fixed
train/dev classes; +3 percentage points so với strongest missing-stream baseline,
clean loss ≤1 point. Chưa registered vì annotation/inputs thiếu. Synthetic masks
chỉ diagnostic; natural failures mới hỗ trợ deployment claims.
Falsifier: dropout/capacity-matched imputation đạt tương đương; reliability
predicts lexeme/signers thay vì observation quality; gain do corpus/label access.

S01 đã stream masking ablations; L01 có articulator masking/prediction collision.
Reliability calibration/natural-failure scope phải có measured difference; chưa
claim novel. Search refresh 2026-10-03, closest robustness review chưa đủ.

Cheapest next action: audit raw stream layout, timestamp synchronization,
confidence/missing masks và provenance trên 2–5 train/dev samples. Keypoint
directory không đủ làm input DINO crop embeddings của SHuBERT. Không đọc/cache
phương pháp cũ để định hướng. Không tạo labels bằng MLLM.
Pilot: zero fill, train-only dropout, imputation, same-capacity reliability;
same cohorts/head/search access. Severity/schema/seed phải predeclare từ dev,
linguistic signal-removal stress tests disclosed riêng. Head/configs/6 total
HPO allocation/seeds/uncertainty/runtime chưa khóa nên measurement forbidden.
No GPU budget consumed/reserved until dependency resolved; total cycle≤4 hours.

Hold triggers: raw inputs/masks/provenance/license missing; scale only after
natural failure evidence và matched controls. Kill mechanism nếu confidence
không có information gain; implementation failure không scientific refutation.
Confirmatory intended: multiple severity/natural slices, ≥3 stochastic seeds,
clean floor, sample grouping và linguistic review khi interpretation đòi hỏi.
Scorecard 3/1/1/1/2, hard blockers thắng tổng điểm. No runs or claims.

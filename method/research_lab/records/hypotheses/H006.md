Question: do class-conditioned residual directions learned from fully labeled
base gloss classes improve dictionary lookup of disjoint local gloss classes
beyond generic unsupervised geometry? Users seek few-example ASL video lookup

falsifies the class-specific explanation. No claim all removed variance is
nuisance or that signer identity causes the effect.

# H003 — Search-bounded annotation and mechanism review

2026-10-04; selected primary sources, not an exhaustive novelty audit. Source cards
document read scope and failed fetches. No reported literature scores are local
baselines. Same-agent review; no independent or linguistic validation.

Decision [D020](../decisions/D020.md): continue annotation feasibility; hold the
natural tracking-failure pilot. The software path is usable on four diagnostic
clips, but compatibility does not establish a scientific problem or improvement.

Native masking, articulator masking, confidence filtering/reconstruction and
simple temporal fill/assignment already have close precedents in S01, L01, S17
and S24. H003 cannot claim novelty from adding these components. A candidate
reliability mechanism must show information beyond observed masks and handedness,
then beat the matched controls in the [intake specification](../protocols/H003_annotation_intake_v1.md).
That contribution is a hypothesis, not established by this search.

S21 provides genuine manual DGS tracking annotations, but TRACKREL001 and
TRACKJOIN001 find no exact or literal-normalized ID matches to current local
PHOENIX2014T train/dev. A small archive prefix supplies no accepted image join.
The labels cannot be attached to this local cohort or ASL by assumption.
CONFSEM001 separately verifies that current exported hand scores classify
handedness; localization/visibility confidence is not exported. These observations
rule out two proposed shortcuts to ground truth, not H003 itself.

S22 remains a project-level lead: fitted real poses and synthetic ground truth
must not be conflated. S23 selected methods introduce another multimodal gating
control lead; its full corruption/annotation and reproducibility details remain
unverified. S25 is an indexed lead after a blocked full-text fetch. None authorizes
a benchmark claim or a numerical control copied from a paper.

The official Boston50 page promises initial-frame handmarks; Boston104 mentions
tracked dominant-hand data. Neither page establishes dense manual annotations
on the local ASL cohort. The newer S24 paper's dense tracking comparison therefore
still needs label-release and coordinate-provenance verification. Public-source
metadata work can continue; automatic bulk download or external registration is
not a solution to the missing join.

Next decision-changing evidence: an existing exact-source annotation path or a
qualified review of the complete fixed diagnostic cohort. The intake specification
defines provenance, visibility, anatomy, geometry, unknown handling and review
order. Do not silently pivot to synthetic stress, detector availability or a
tracking-only task to produce a positive result. H002's existing narrow efficiency
analysis remains available for contribution review under its fixed policies.

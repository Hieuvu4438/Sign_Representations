Decision input, not novelty certification. Sources: [S13](sources/S13.md),
[S17](sources/S17.md), previous matrix/cards. Same agent sequential reviews.

Researcher: existing sign embeddings already support few-shot similarity and
linear probes. H001 must first measure the local pooling failure and compare
simple frozen controls; label scarcity alone does not supply a contribution.

Engineer: include cosine nearest-prototype/neighbor and regularized linear
probe candidates in the exact pilot design. Do not silently copy SignCLIP's
unusual `n_neighbors=#classes` into a different support budget or vocabulary;
its implementation must be audited if paper reproduction is claimed.

Protocol Auditor: S13's text supervision, pose pipeline, ten-shot support and
frame exclusions differ from our setting. S17 fine-tunes target-task encoders;
synthetic hand reconstruction is not natural missing-stream recognition.
No paper-reported scores serve as local matched-cohort baselines.

Statistician: support sampling and optimization variability require separate
reporting. Three supports on six dev participants will not establish broad
signer-population generalization. Pilot floor remains proposed, not observed.

Skeptical Reviewer: ordered-bin pooling is a diagnostic, not a novel module.
Without order-specific evidence against shuffled/capacity controls, kill that
mechanism claim. Continue H001 prerequisites; hold H003 raw-stream measurement.
Next novelty query: sign-specific ordered temporal pooling/frozen low-label
probes, then general video temporal pyramids/order controls before REGISTER.

# S32 — Nonlinear kernel nuisance attribute projection

Xianyu Zhao, Yuan Dong, Jian Zhao, Liang Lu, Jiqing Liu, Haila Wang (2008),
*Comparison of Input and Feature Space Nonlinear Kernel Nuisance Attribute
Projections for Speaker Verification*, Interspeech2008,1377–1380.
[Institutional publisher PDF](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/25682215/i08_1377.pdf),
access2026-10-04. Selected title/intro/§3.1 Eq4–6; other constructions/results
not fully reviewed, code/checkpoints unknown. Browser PDF, no local page anchors.

NAP removes a learned subspace using P=I-UU^T with orthonormal directions.
The reviewed formulation chooses leading eigenvectors of a weighted within-
speaker pair-variation matrix to reduce channel/session variation. This is
prior art for subspace removal, not an ASL signer-specific implementation or
evidence all participant variability is harmful. Our participant residual means
and cosine lookup differ from the SVM/kernel criterion; no reproduction claim.

The encoder is trained with a sign classifier and competing signer classifier;
the objective encourages uniform signer predictions (or empirical prior when
imbalanced). An added penalty compares signer means at encoder layers. This
establishes prior signer-invariance/mean-matching mechanisms. The datasets include
MKLM ASL and SI-PSL Portuguese isolated signs, plus Jochen-Triesch hand gestures;
they are not ASL Citizen video retrieval. Code/checkpoint/compute and exact
implementation of printed losses remain unaudited; extraction has equation
formatting ambiguities, no formula copied into implementation. No numerical
local baseline/causal invariance/privacy claim. H005_v1's frozen projection
diagnostic is different but not novel merely because it uses SignRep.

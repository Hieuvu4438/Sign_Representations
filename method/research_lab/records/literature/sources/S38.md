# S38 — Standard Ledoit-Wolf implementation

[Official sklearn API](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.covariance.LedoitWolf.html),
access2026-10-04; online docs1.9.1, local installed implementation1.8.0.
Software source, not an extra paper. Parameters/Notes/source selected.

Covariance is shrunk toward trace(C)/d times identity; coefficient is estimated
from the input rather than a dev grid. `assume_centered=True` omits another mean
subtraction. Local source _shrunk_covariance.py/_ledoit_wolf and estimator,
plus empirical_covariance imported implementation, read/hashed for H006_v1.
An independent small fourth-moment oracle checks covariance/coefficient and
whitening identity. No package install/environment modification.

Local global pooled residuals share estimated class means and signer groups,
so the coefficient is a standard data-derived control, not a proof of optimal
estimation under IID sampling. Denominator N matches this implementation,
not unbiased N-minus-classes scatter. Two ranking metrics (cosine after L2 and
unscaled Mahalanobis) fixed before the run, not selected from target results.

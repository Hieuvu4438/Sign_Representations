# S37 — FROST

Junghwan Park. *FROST: Training-Free Few-Shot Segmentation with Frozen Features
and Nonparametric Statistics*. [Primary arXiv2606.31136v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.31136v1),
[metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31136),submitted2026-06-30; access2026-10-04.
Preprint; venue unverified. Selected§3.1–3.2/Eq1 and AppendixA.3,
not complete results/code review; no segmentation reproduction.

Frozen DINOv3 tokens receive positional projection and within-class shrinkage
whitening. Foreground/background support-mask classes supply residual scatter;
scaled-identity shrinkage weight.95 and inverse-square-root transform condition
geometry. The text explicitly distinguishes within-class scatter from total
covariance to preserve between-class mean differences. AppendixA.3 also specifies
a diagonal floor1e-4. Subsequent density/spatial decisions differ from retrieval.

This directly limits novelty of class-conditioned frozen-feature reweighting.
Our fully labeled old-class fit/new-class clip lookup is different access/task,
not a novel covariance operation. H006_v1 uses automatic Ledoit-Wolf/no floor,
not FROST replication or its fixed.95 setting. Code link uninspected.

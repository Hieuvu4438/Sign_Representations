# S29 — UPRet

Xuan Wu et al., *Uncertainty-aware Sign Language Video Retrieval with Probability
Distribution Modeling*, ECCV2024.
[Official ECVA PDF](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/06074.pdf),
accessed2026-10-04. Selected§3.3–3.5/Eq8–12 and title/metadata; not full code/eval audit.
Paper names [author repository](https://github.com/xua222/UPRet), not inspected here.

Video/text features parameterize distributions, sampled embeddings feed optimal
transport matching, and paired cross-modal supervision trains retrieval. This
uncertainty concerns semantic alignment, distinct from unencoded finite query
windows. It does not establish a certificate for stopping a frozen dictionary
query. Learned distributions or the word “uncertainty” are not new sign-method
contributions. No local reproduction, numeric comparison or linguistic conclusion
drawn from the paper. Repository/checkpoints, exact splits and compute remain
unaudited; follow up only if a proposed method actually uses this mechanism.

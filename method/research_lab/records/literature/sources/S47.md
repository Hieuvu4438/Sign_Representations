# S47 — Open-Set Face Identification on Few-Shot Gallery by Fine-Tuning

Hojin Park; Jaewoo Park; Andrew Beng Jin Teoh.
[arXiv2301.01922v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.01922),2023-01-05;
[primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.01922),access2026-10-05.
Selected §III-D Eq3–4 and §IV-A. Venue/code/checkpoint/commit unknown.

Identification uses cosine to class prototypes. NAC rescales similarity using
the exponentiated scores of the query's top-k gallery neighbors. Eq4 therefore
already supplies a gallery-relative rejection score. §IV-A splits known/unknown
identities and forms a few-image gallery; the broader method also fine-tunes
BatchNorm layers after classifier weight imprinting. Frozen NAC scoring alone
must not be called a reproduction of that full adaptation pipeline.

Inputs are face images; this supplies mechanism prior art, not sign-domain
utility. Exact evaluator/tie/threshold fitting, authors' parameter search,
training cost and local comparability have not been audited. No scores imported.
H007: any proposed top-neighbor normalization needs NAC as a matched scoring
control; differences in encoder/dataset alone do not establish novelty.
Grade: selected primary computational methods; author/COI and peer-review
process unknown. Follow-up: pin evaluator/code before implementation/parity.

# S28 — RT-STAMP-SLR

S. Renjith, Aneesh Varghese, S. S. Poorna; Discover Artificial Intelligence6,
article713, published2026-05-26.
[Publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44163-026-01429-3),
accessed2026-10-04; selected§3.1.3/3.1.7 and limitations, not full code/data audit.

Motion-energy thresholds choose frames; a sustained classifier-confidence
threshold triggers an early decision. A buffered temporal model supplies the
classification output. Adaptive frame selection and confidence stopping therefore
already exist in sign recognition. These heuristics are not evidence of a formal
unchanged-dense-retrieval guarantee. Different supervision/input/classification
setting prevents direct numeric comparison to H004. Dataset release, split,
confidence calibration and full runtime comparability remain unverified; no
paper score or speed treated as a matched local baseline.

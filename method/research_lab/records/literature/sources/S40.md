# S40 — MS-ASL metadata, split and evaluator specification

Hamid Reza Vaezi Joze; Oscar Koller. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.01053v2),
arXiv1812.01053v2, 2019-11-20; selected sections3.2–3.5 and initial section4,
access2026-10-04. Not a full results/code review.

The paper reports English synonym/polysemy handling, face-cluster/manual signer
identification, 165/37/20 train/validation/test signers, four vocabulary subsets,
and mean-per-class Top1/Top5 evaluation. The 1000-class annotation counts are
16054/5287/4172. Signer boxes and 64-frame baseline inputs are reported; the
author's short-clip repetition is not a SignRep input contract. Written English
labels alone do not establish a cross-corpus sign-form ontology. These are
reported specifications, pending direct release/local checks.

[Publisher release](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/download/details.aspx?id=100121)
lists August2019 version, published2024-07-15, MS-ASL.zip approximately1.9MB.
[Public archive](https://download.microsoft.com/download/3/c/a/3ca92c78-1c4a-4a91-a7ee-6980c1d242ec/MS-ASL.zip)
is the planned identity reference, not yet downloaded at registration. No
evaluator-code parity or official score reproduction. Adapted dictionary
retrieval requires its own protocol; no ontology or checkpoint-use inference.

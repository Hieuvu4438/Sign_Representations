Yan Wang, Wei-Lun Chao, Kilian Q. Weinberger, Laurens van der Maaten,
*SimpleShot: Revisiting Nearest-Neighbor Classification for Few-Shot Learning*,
arXiv1911.04623v2(2019); venue not checked. Access2026-10-04.

Selected§2/2.1 and initial§3.1, not complete results/code or full review.

The classifier uses a base-class-trained encoder and nearest neighbors for
one-shot/centroids for multi-shot. Centering uses the base mean; L2 normalization
then affects the nearest-neighbor geometry. This is a strong simple-transformation
precedent, not evidence our frozen unit-pooled SignRep pipeline reproduces their
supervised image encoder/episodes. Paper names https://github.com/mileyan/simple_shot
as code, not audited here. No reported scores used as local controls.
H006 includes fixed centering/normalization and comparable base-data access,
but native feature regime/ASL dictionary/corpus/splits differ. Such differences
alone do not create method novelty. Full code/protocol/compute audit unknown.

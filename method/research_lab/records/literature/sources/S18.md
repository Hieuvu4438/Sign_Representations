# S18 — Spatial and Temporal Pyramids for Grammatical Expression Recognition

Nicholas Michael, Dimitris Metaxas, Carol Neidle; ASSETS2009.
[Author-hosted primary PDF](https://www.bu.edu/asllrp/stp.pdf), accessed2026-10-04
local; title/authors/venue on first page. Reading selected §4.3 and §5,
not all tracking derivations or an implementation audit.

Verified: yaw-angle derivatives quantized using a training-derived codebook;
temporal cells/pyramid with match kernel and SVM detect negative expressions.
Three levels tested; more levels hurt reported performance. ASLLRP close-up
face videos and signer-separated train/test, binary expression tasks; this is
not frozen RGB isolated-sign low-label evaluation.

Implication/inference: temporal partitioning already exists in sign-language
work, including non-manual expression analysis. Coarse bins with a new encoder
are not alone novel. H001 must remain controlled empirical analysis with
order-destroying/capacity controls. Reported score cannot serve as matched
baseline for ASL Citizen. Source split IDs/code availability unknown.

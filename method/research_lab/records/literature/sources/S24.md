# S24 — Optical-flow and previous-state hand tracking

Jawas, Tjahjadi; IEEEAccess2026,14,30125–30142; DOI10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3667503. [Institutional metadata](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/198992/), [published PDF](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/198992/1/Unsupervised_Hand_Tracking_for_Sign_Language_Recognition_via_Optical_Flow_and_Previous_Tracking_Data.pdf), accessed2026-10-04. Selected tracking equations24–34, §III-A/metrics37–38; no code/full release audit.

Optical flow generates candidates; prior state, holding missing locations and ID-swap checks stabilize two tracks. Evaluation compares localization/tracking error and speed with MediaPipe/other detectors on Boston/Phoenix/BOBSL; some comparative accuracy values are inherited from earlier publications.

Relevant simpler temporal tracking baseline; anti-duplication, previous-frame fill and ID swapping are not novel alone. Hand-location evidence does not establish downstream linguistic quality. Dense GT coverage/release and all coordinate conventions still require verification; Boston50 official page only promises initial-frame handmarks.

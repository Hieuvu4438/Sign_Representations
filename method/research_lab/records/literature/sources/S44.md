Postprocessing whitens matching-pair scatter, rotates by whitened nonmatching
scatter, optionally truncates, then normalizes centered descriptors. Pair labels
come from3D reconstruction, unlike H006's explicit class labels. This is direct
retrieval precedent for within-class whitening versus total PCA.
Own algebraic inference: with full dimensions an orthogonal post-whitening
rotation preserves cosine; changing covariance eigen-directions in H006 changes
the metric instead. No paper claim of the local matched-spectrum ablation.
Authors' [project](https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/cnnimageretrieval/) located; code/version
not inspected or run. Novel whitening math claim unsupported; own independent
sign-corpus analysis utility still requires results and contribution audit.

# S26 — BanditMIPS

Tiwari et al., *Faster Maximum Inner Product Search in High Dimensions*;
[arXiv2212.07551v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.07551v3), accessed2026-10-04.
Selected§2/3/3.2/Algorithm1; venue and released code not independently audited.

Inner products become arm means estimated by coordinate acquisition. Confidence
intervals eliminate candidates; unresolved search falls back to exact computation.
Bounds require justified tail/scale assumptions, not estimated softmax confidence.
This is direct prior art for adaptive score acquisition and stopping; such ideas
are not new merely applied to signs. H004 acquires expensive shared encoder windows
and uses weighted/class-max scores, rather than cheap stored-vector coordinates.
That cost/aggregation difference is a candidate distinction, not established
novelty. CERTBOUND/CERTPAIR use deterministic completion envelopes, not a reproduced
BanditMIPS implementation or paper runtime comparison.

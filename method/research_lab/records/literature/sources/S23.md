# S23 — UniModal-LSR

Hoang et al.; CMC2026,88(2),35; [publisher full text](https://www.techscience.com/cmc/v88n2/67609/html), DOI10.32604/cmc.2026.078743. Access2026-10-04. Selected§3.1/3.3, narrativeTable10, §7 and materials statement; table-image values not read.

Bidirectional lip/sign attention with adaptive gating, supervised task objectives and semantic alignment. Cross-dataset synthetic pair construction uses text matching; this supervision differs from frozen SHuBERT. Authors discuss missing/degraded modalities and substantial training cost, while recognizing semantic-pair noise.

Gating/missing-modality evaluation already exist; no first-robustness claim. Natural-tracking ground truth, exact corruption generator, method code/checkpoint and fair local reproduction remain unverified. Do not use reported scores as baselines or gloss/text overlap as sign-form equivalence.

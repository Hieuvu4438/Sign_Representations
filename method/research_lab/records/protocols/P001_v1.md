## Metric specification

| Metric | Role | Algorithm / scale | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Top-1 | H001 proposed primary | fraction of correct rank ≤1, micro per query, 0–1 | eval_metrics |
| Top-5/10/20 | secondary diagnostic | rank ≤ min(k,C), complete ranking | eval_metrics |
| MRR | secondary | mean 1/r, first/only correct class, 0–1 | eval_metrics |
| DCG | secondary | mean 1/log2(r+1), binary single-class relevance, no normalization | eval_metrics |

Rank one-based; exactly one relevant class/query. All-wrong top1 gives zero
top1, but MRR/DCG >0 when relevant class ranks lower; empty prediction rejected.
Tie rule: descending score then ascending class ID, local deterministic choice.
Official torch argsort tie ordering not established. No nDCG substitution,
no per-instance gallery Recall naming, no macro accuracy claim from this scorer.
Strict sample-ID join: completeness/uniqueness/mapping and finite scores before
ranking. Aggregation fraction; 100× conversion only at reporting with units.
Uncertainty proposal: paired participant bootstrap on dev, 2.000 replicates,
seed 1701; only six dev participants, so population claim limited. Optimization
and support-subset variation must be reported separately. Final plan not frozen.

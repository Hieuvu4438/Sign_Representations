## Fixed representations and controls before performance feedback

- Output **features**768, post-LayerNorm/linear z_avg; encoder frozen/eval.
- Mean, max, activity weighting, ordered, source-shuffled and projected-mean.
- Activity weights max of two raw regression estimates clamped[0,1]; sum≤1e-12
  falls back to clip mean and is counted. Smooth-L1 training/source audit in
  ACTIVITY_CONTRACT.md; this adapted bound/fallback is not exact native pooling.
- Four ordered means over relative window-center range; boundary floor/clamp,
  empty bin filled by clip mean with bin counts recorded. No duration-weighting.
- Concatenated4×768→768 Gaussian projection, numpy default_rng1337, entries
  normal std1/sqrt768. No fitting/PCA/projection-seed selection. Store code,
  NumPy version and matrix hash; reconstruct matrix deterministically.
- Shuffled control permutes bins independently per source SHA256 using seed2718
  hashed into local RNG. No labels/gloss-containing IDs/split information in
  permutation key. A single global bin permutation would not destroy consistent
  order and is deliberately excluded from this falsifier.
- Projected-mean repeats mean4 times and uses the same projection, controlling
  static transformation/output capacity. All pooled outputs remain768D.
- All head inputs normalized per sample; reject zero/nonfinite vectors or
  missing sources. No target-dev corpus normalization/fitting.

## Candidate first-pilot plan

For all six pooling variants use deterministic ridge classification, alpha1.0,
intercept through support-only X/one-hotY centering, minimize squared error plus
alpha×weight norm. Same output dimensions/head/regularization for comparisons.
Simple frozen cosine nearest-gallery class-max controls for mean/max/activity;
class-max scores cover100 unique classes. This class collapse is a declared
adaptation, not an unaudited official gallery evaluator claim.

No HPO in this first failure pilot (0/6 trials). No stochastic training seeds;
all three support subsets used and reported separately at1/2/4/8 labels.
Fixed alpha may be suboptimal; before a method/scale claim, allocate remaining
fair search budget and confirm that weak regularization did not drive a gain.
Do not label fixed defaults as a tuned strongest baseline.

Primary: mean micro Top1 across2/4-label budgets and all three support seeds.
Compare ordered-ridge to strongest aggregate primary score among mean/max/
activity/projected-mean ridge and three nearest-gallery controls. Secondary:
full label curve, Top5/10/20, MRR, DCG; scorer/ties unchanged from VAL001/v2.
Every dev ID gets complete unique-class ranking; labels joined by ID.

Provisional practical signal: ordered gain≥2pp over strongest baseline AND
ordered-minus-shuffled≥1pp, with positive ordered-baseline difference for≥2
of3 support seeds. These thresholds predate any performance result and are
budget-driven screening thresholds, not statistically powered equivalence.
Absence does not by itself prove representation equivalence.

Uncertainty: mean/SD and all support-seed scores; paired participant bootstrap
2000 replicates, seed1701,95% interval of primary difference conditional on
dev-selected strongest baseline. Six dev participants; exploratory interval
does not correct baseline/model selection or establish broad population utility.
No best-seed headline or inferential significance/novelty claim.

Pre-feedback implementation clarification2026-10-04: strongest-baseline ties
resolve lexicographically by method name. Bootstrap uses percentile quantiles
and recomputes micro accuracy over clips in participant groups sampled with
replacement, preserving unequal group sizes and sharing draws across all
methods/support seeds/budgets. It does not average participant accuracies.

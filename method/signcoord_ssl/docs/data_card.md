# Local data and target provenance

The current inventory is in `docs/evidence/local_inventory.json`, generated by
`tools/inventory_local.py`. Counts are filesystem/metadata availability, not
whole-corpus decode verification.

- How2Sign local train: 31,047 MP4 and 31,047 paired pose filenames.
- ASL-Citizen: 83,399 MP4 and pose filenames. CSV counts 40,154 train / 10,304
  val / 32,941 test; signer sets 35 / 6 / 11 have no intersections in the CSV.
- WLASL local: 21,095 paired MP4/pose filenames. Do not infer benchmark class
  coverage from the directory name.

The implementation never reads gloss/caption/class/signer as SSL inputs or
objectives. `student_inputs` and `targets` are separate. Label maps are explicit
downstream evaluator inputs. Frozen feature extraction requires no pose cache.

## Legacy cache tracing

The actual reader in `method/signrep_spectral/data/pose_reader.py` and its
discovery record describe image-normalized `(x/W,y/H)` coordinates and unknown
backend scores. `method/p4_signssl/p4/cip_states.py` calls these unnormalized
scores and fits part thresholds; it does not identify the original estimator.
The official SignRep repository supplies RGB inference and prior heads, not
the producer of this particular local `.pkl` cache.

`tools/trace_legacy_pose.py` generated 50 train-clip candidate overlays (25
ASL-Citizen, 25 How2Sign) at a single-person middle frame. All ten contact sheets
were visually inspected. `(x*W,y*H)` aligns face/body/hand positions; same-axis
W/W or H/H scales visibly misalign non-square videos. Pose accuracy is imperfect
on occluded/crossed hands. This verifies the coordinate conversion hypothesis
on these frames; it does not prove every trajectory or extractor identity.

Legacy arrays are ragged `[F][P_t,133,2]`, with `[P_t,133]` scores. Zero/multiple
persons invalidate the frame. This fixes an initial inventory assumption of a
fixed one-person dimension. The initial reported inspection error was a ragged
array conversion error, not a corrupt video.

## Versioned pilot conversion

`tools/prepare_legacy_pilot.py` prepares 100 SSL-train and 20 source-group-disjoint
SSL-dev clips from How2Sign official train. Source recordings found in local
How2Sign official eval/test are excluded. Original data stays read-only.

Quality policy `legacy_image_normalized_xy_train_part_q90_quality_v1` maps raw
positive scores to `clip(score / train-only part q90,0,1)`, for body/face/L/R.
This is an explicitly heuristic bounded quality score, not a probability and
not evidence of localization calibration. Calibration JSON includes all train
pose hashes. Each canonical NPZ keeps raw scores, converted pixel XY, observed
and status masks, decoded source PTS and provenance; key includes media, pose,
schema, quality calibration and policy hashes.

Unknowns retained: original pose estimator/config/weights; true track identity;
independent original pose-frame timestamps; SSL signer IDs; SignRep's original
pretraining overlap with the downstream datasets. Canonical semantic order is
inferred from the previous reader and overlays, while the canonical names are
pinned to official MMPose metadata. Near-duplicate screening is completed only
for the selected screening manifests described below; the full-corpus audit remains pending.
`signer_disjoint_verified=false` remains visible. These prevent a claim of fully
verified strict-inductive evaluation at present.

## PTS tolerance correction

The first 100-clip audit showed normalization 100/100, mean geometry validity
0.96094, but only 9 positive eligible spectra per pair/bin. The source median
FPS was 24 while requested grid FPS was 25. Using half the *requested* interval
(20ms) as tolerance discarded valid nearest frames at up to 20.83ms source
quantization error, often invalidating both complete windows.

Policy v2 uses half the median *source* PTS interval as the default tolerance;
the 10% repeat-fraction exclusion and basic validity stay intact. Derivatives
and FFT use the uniform requested grid, with nearest-source mapping recorded.
A dedicated 24-to-25 Hz regression test checks this. This is a sampler correction
before any frozen benchmark measurement, not a test-fitted gate relaxation.
Version-1 reports/checkpoints remain as diagnostics; version-2 stats are refit
on the same train IDs and saved under new paths.

Version 3 also separates the crop-grid origin from the allowed manifest source
interval; otherwise its nearest earlier source frame was discarded incorrectly.
Positive calibration observations increased 9 → 44 → 93 per pair/bin without
changing confidence thresholds or train IDs. Final visibility policy additionally
rejects joints outside the original camera bounds before affine; this avoids
validating unseen joints in letterbox padding and leaves 92 observations per
pair/bin. These historical paths use `target_stats_bounds_v4.json` and
`phase_calibration_bounds_v4.json`. Current training requires the v8 recipe-bound
statistics described below. A4/v4 here labels an audit artifact revision,
not the A4 Parseval experiment arm.

## Production adapters and selected downstream cohorts

`manifest` now supports ASL-Citizen, WLASL2000, How2Sign and the explicit
`configured_source` route. Labels are written separately. Every selected video
is decoded completely to verify strict PTS and constant geometry, hashed and
indexed under this project's cache; failures and selection hashes are recorded.
Stable file fingerprints avoid redundant media hashing/PTS scans within a
process. Mutation invalidates the frozen media/index contract.

The ASL-Citizen screen is metadata-selected before evaluation: 50 classes,
200 official-train gallery videos (4/class), 100 official-val/dev queries
(2/class), 23/5 known signers, and no decode failures. Gallery/query media,
source/session and known signer intersections are zero. Test predictions are
not read. This is a subset screen, not the full official benchmark.

The WLASL screen initially has 200 train gallery videos and 86 dev queries,
50 shared classes, and no decode failures. Official upstream annotations and
preprocessing are pinned to ac00e6be631c1a2a486621b65f202219f3964d6b.
Local clips are already trimmed; original 1-based annotation frame intervals
are retained as provenance and never applied again to local MP4 PTS. The
21,095 local IDs and 21,083 upstream IDs are not assumed identical; unresolved
IDs must fail in the extraction ledger if selected. Original URL defines the
conservative source group. Three selected gallery videos (15473,42662,33765)
share dev source groups and are excluded into a separate frozen
`train_source_filtered.jsonl` (197 videos, all 50 classes). Known signer overlap
remains: this secondary protocol cannot be described as signer-held-out.

`screen_overlap_perceptual_reviewed_v1.json` screens the 100 How2Sign SSL-train
clips against 20 SSL-dev plus the selected ASL-Citizen/WLASL dev cohorts. Exact
source checks pass. Five uniform keyframes, 64-bit DCT pHash, Hamming≤6 and ≥3
distinct monotonic matched frames produce three candidates, all within How2Sign.
Each was visually reviewed as different content, with image/frame hashes and
explicit decisions in `perceptual_review_decisions_v1.json`. Similar studio
backgrounds are the observed reason for these candidates. This bounded screen
does not prove absence of partial reuse, does not identify signers, and does
not replace the full downstream dev/test audit.

## Source gaps and canonical pose content binding

Interpolation occurs before affine/normalization for at most two source frames,
only when integer track IDs are explicitly verified and identical across the
gap/endpoints. Both endpoints need confidence≥.30, allowed missing statuses are
1/2, and reliability is half the lower endpoint quality. Out-of-camera joints,
track changes, unknown identities, absent people and padding are not filled.
The legacy How2Sign cohort has unknown tracks, so interpolation stays disabled.
Synthetic verified/changed/unknown/out-of-frame integration cases enforce this.

Current statistics bind the source-gap policy, geometry/spectral recipe
(normalization, spatial size and repeat eligibility), and the aggregate SHA256
of all canonical train NPZ files in addition to manifest/schema/timing policies.
Stable-stat per-read checks catch ordinary pose-file mutation after dataset
initialization; recomputing the dataset hash rejects old statistics after a
mutation. Current paths are `target_stats_recipe_bound_v8.json` and
`phase_calibration_recipe_bound_v8.json` inside `data/how2sign_pilot_v1/stats`.
Older v4/v5/v6/v7 statistics are historical diagnostics and fail current validation.
The 100 fixed train IDs still give 92 positive eligible observations per pair/bin.
The statistic fitting crop seed is recorded explicitly and remains shared across
matched arms; later training seeds do not silently change fitted thresholds.

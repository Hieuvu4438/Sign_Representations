## Critical analysis

- Author limitations (Conclusion): glosses costly; no complete replacement of annotations; unified framework may be beaten by task-specific methods; future RGB/depth, SMPL-X for occlusion robustness.
- INFERENCE: gains attributable to text supervision + mT5 capacity; cannot be used as SSL-only evidence. Whole-sequence encoder but temporal order handling not analysed; noise handling not described (no confidence use found).
- Nuisance: argues skeletons "abstract away background clutter and appearance variations" (§4) — assertion, not tested.

## Liên hệ project

- Out-of-family comparator: shows what text supervision buys on WLASL2000 under FT. Do not compare to SignRep frozen retrieval.

## Critical analysis

- Discrete targets (like SHuBERT clusters) on pose; frame-wise tokenization ignores motion (critique by MASA P108 §I: "recovering the static information").
- No confidence handling found in extracted text; in-domain pretraining; FT only.

## Liên hệ project

- Prior art for discrete/clustered targets on sign pose (pre-SHuBERT). Relevant if considering tokenised pose-prior targets instead of SignRep's regression.

## Mechanism

- MoCo contrastive per modality with spatial (rotation, scale, joint mask, flip) and temporal (random crop + resample) augmentations; consistency between hand and trunk embeddings; cross-modal neighbour-based knowledge transfer between joint and motion spaces (§III).
- Motion branch (first-order differences) is claimed to reduce "interference of differences in spatial position" across signers (§I).

## Critical analysis

- Strengths: one of few works reporting frozen linear-eval numbers for itself AND for SignBERT/SignBERT+/BEST under one protocol (Table V).
- INFERENCE: pretraining on the training splits of all evaluation datasets (in-domain) — frozen numbers are not cross-corpus generalisation.
- Temporal: whole-sign 64-frame encoder; motion branch adds explicit dynamics; temporal crop+resample augmentation pushes speed invariance; no order/shuffle diagnostic.
- Nuisance: pose modality + motion differences framed as signer-variation remedy; no signer-split test.
- Pose noise: not explicitly handled (joint mask is an augmentation, not reliability weighting).
- Limitations section: not found in extracted text (UNKNOWN).

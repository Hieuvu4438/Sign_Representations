## Critical analysis

- INFERENCE: WLASL top-1 conflates model error with label inconsistency; a frozen representation that groups the same sign under different English glosses gets penalized. Retrieval "errors" on WLASL may be correct sign matches.
- No quantified error rate across all 2,000 glosses in the parts read.

## Liên hệ project

- For WLASL secondary results, consider reporting on the revised-label subset as a supplemental diagnostic; at minimum, cite label inconsistency as a known limitation.
- Cheap check: merge revised labels and measure how many WLASL2000 class pairs collapse to one ASLLRP sign.

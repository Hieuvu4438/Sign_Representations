## Mechanism

- Gradient path in Stage 2: student and predictor only. The teacher is frozen, so there is no stop-grad/EMA and no collapse mode by construction (§2.2). The student loss correlates with downstream accuracy (R² 0.951 / 0.972 / 0.984 for 80k/40k/20k teachers, Fig. 3).
- Why it works (authors): a stable target. Even weak teachers yield students better than themselves ("weak-teacher, strong-student", §6).
- The prediction target is the teacher's own masked tokens of the same clip. There is no cross-clip or future-clip target. Masks span the full temporal axis.

## Critical analysis

- Strengths: clean controls (same data, same steps). Teacher-size, teacher-data and masking ablations. Interpretable loss.
- Author-stated limitations (§7): what makes a good teacher is not explained, the mechanism of extra student stages is unclear, and scaling plateaus, likely due to data limits.
- INFERENCE: no time-reversal or order evaluation. "Temporal understanding" is inferred from SSv2 accuracy only. With full-time tube masks, the latent target is a within-clip spatial-region prediction. There is no explicit future objective.
- INFERENCE: Table 15's causal-mask result is a warning sign for future-only objectives *in Stage 1* (pixel teacher). It is not evidence about cross-window latent prediction with a strong frozen teacher.

## Overlap with H1/H2

- H2. What SALT already does:
  - frozen-teacher latent prediction (no EMA) as the student objective, which is H2's "predict a frozen teacher's embedding" design;
  - the evidence that small or out-of-domain frozen teachers suffice, which supports using frozen SignRep as teacher;
  - interpretability of the loss.
- What SALT does not do:
  - domain: no sign language;
  - training regime: from-scratch pretraining at scale, not continued pretraining of an existing checkpoint;
  - target: same-clip masked tokens, not a future or other window;
  - anchor: no self-distillation anchor to preserve the original features;
  - evaluation regime: frozen attentive probes for classification only, no retrieval/kNN;
  - order: no reversal or direction measurement.
- So H2's "frozen teacher" component is not novel. It is SALT and MVD-style. The only possible delta is (i) **cross-window future target** + (ii) continued pretraining with an anchor in the sign domain + (iii) direction-sensitivity and retrieval evaluation. (i) has a mixed-to-negative prior: V-JEPA's causal-mask ablation (P201 Table 4), SALT Table 15, and FRAME's anticipation giving no gain on classification (P406).
- H1: no overlap (H1 doesn't train the backbone).
- Separating experiment: continued pretraining from SignRep with a frozen SignRep teacher, steps-matched, comparing (a) future-window target vs (b) same-window masked-token target (SALT-style) vs (c) no new loss. Primary readouts: reversal cosine (per-window and pooled) and ASL Citizen R@1 with **both** weighted-mean and ordered 2-bin readouts. Rule 20 requires showing the gain is in the encoder, not just the head. If (b) ≈ (a), the "future" story adds nothing beyond SALT.

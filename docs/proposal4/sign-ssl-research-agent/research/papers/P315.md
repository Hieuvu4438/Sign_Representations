## Critical analysis

- Only dataset in the anchor's evaluation suite explicitly built around non-manual (face/mouth) distinctions → diagnostic for whether a hand-pose-teacher SSL representation encodes non-manual cues.
- INFERENCE: 2 test signers → very small signer sample; repeated takes per signer (3) mean within-signer near-duplicates across train if a random split were used (the official split is signer-independent).
- Chinese Sign Language — results do not transfer as claims about ASL.

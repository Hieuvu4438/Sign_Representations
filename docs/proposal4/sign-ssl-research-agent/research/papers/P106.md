## Mechanism

- Same as SignBERT plus clip-level masking (contiguous frames) → forces temporal dynamics modelling over short spans; spatial-temporal PE.
- Noise handling: confidence-threshold exclusion in loss (§III) + masking designed to mimic detector failures.

## Critical analysis

- Strengths: explicit noise-mimicking masking; user study on denoising.
- Author-acknowledged failure: pose detection fails due to "partially occluded upper body, motion blur and complex background" (§IV, CSLR discussion).
- INFERENCE: still 32-frame sampling for ISLR, in-domain pretraining, fine-tuned eval only.

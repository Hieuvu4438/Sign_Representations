## Critical analysis

- Relevance: shows a sign-specific Hiera MAE can be trained with 128-frame clips (8× SignRep's 16-frame windows) and used frozen. SignRep chose 16-frame windows + segment averaging; SSVP-SLT is the closest RGB-only evidence that longer temporal units are feasible.
- Not evaluated on ISLR/dictionary retrieval (INFERENCE from setup; not exhaustively checked in results).
- Face blur reduces non-manual information (authors discuss in §2).

## Liên hệ project

- H-seed-A: baseline for "longer temporal unit" in RGB sign MAE. H-seed-D: blur trade-off evidence.

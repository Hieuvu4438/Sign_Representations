## Critical analysis

- Author limitation: depends on off-the-shelf detectors/segmentation/keypoints; pipeline maximises SNR "not to eliminate every possible imperfection" (§3).
- INFERENCE: no frozen evaluation; no ASL Citizen / MS-ASL; in-domain pretraining; transition-frame trimming by heuristic is analogous to SignRep's activity weighting.
- Nuisance: segmentation-guided masking reduces background reconstruction; not a nuisance-invariance test.

## Liên hệ project

- Prior art for hand-region-focused MAE masking in RGB sign SSL (relevant to any SignRep masking change). Heuristic removal of non-signing frames parallels SignRep's activity-weighted pooling.

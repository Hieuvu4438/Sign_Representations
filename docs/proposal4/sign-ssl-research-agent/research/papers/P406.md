## Mechanism

- Anticipation decoders force the current-frame representation to carry information predictive of near-future teacher features. Memory supplies past context. Gradients reach only the memory module and decoders in the chosen setting.
- This is causal (past → future) latent prediction against a frozen teacher, the same family as H2's target design, but at frame level with an image teacher.

## Critical analysis

- Author limitation (§5): no fine-tuning evaluation. Future work "to better encode fine-grained motion and long-term dynamics".
- INFERENCE: anticipation helped dense correspondence, not clip-level classification. That is mild negative prior evidence for "future prediction → better clip-level (retrieval) embeddings". No time-reversal or direction evaluation. No sign data.
- Selection risk: loss weights tuned on a DAVIS subset, and DAVIS is also reported.

## Overlap with H1/H2

- H2. What FRAME already does:
  - predicting a frozen teacher's *future* features from current+past context as a self-supervised signal on top of a pretrained encoder;
  - a two-stage design: a frozen Stage-1 encoder plus trained temporal modules.
- What FRAME does not do:
  - granularity: frame and patch level with image teachers, not window/clip level with a video teacher;
  - training target: Stage 2 does not update the backbone in its chosen variant; H2 updates the backbone;
  - anchor: no anchor or self-distillation constraint;
  - domain: no sign language;
  - evaluation: no retrieval of clip embeddings and no reversal or direction test.
- H1: partial. The memory module over frozen features is a causal temporal head on frozen features, but its output is per-frame, not a single sign vector.
- Delta for H2 vs FRAME: window-level future latent prediction *updating the video backbone*, for sign retrieval, evaluated on direction sensitivity. The mechanism (future-teacher-feature prediction) is not novel. FRAME Table 5 suggests its benefit concentrates on dense/temporal-consistency tasks rather than clip classification.
- Separating experiment: in H2 runs, also train a FRAME-style variant (frozen SignRep backbone + small causal temporal head predicting the next window's frozen embedding, head discarded or used as aggregator). If backbone updating (H2) does not beat this frozen-backbone variant on reversal sensitivity and R@1, H2's backbone training is unjustified compute.

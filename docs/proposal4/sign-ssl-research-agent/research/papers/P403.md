## Mechanism (the paper's account, plus a check)

- Measurement: reversal keeps every frame and spatial feature and only flips order, so a frozen encoder that changes its embedding, and whose probe flips to the antonym class, "internalised" direction (§8).
- Authors' causal story (§8): "During pretraining, V-JEPA masks a contiguous block of future patches and predicts their latent representations from earlier context, creating an inherently directional learning signal". They also say contrastive learning "suppresses this axis by treating temporal augmentations as positive pairs" and pixel reconstruction "dilutes it".
- CHECK against primary sources (INFERENCE, important): V-JEPA's multi-block masks extend over the whole temporal axis ("tube"; P201 note §3.2/App. C). SALT copies the V-JEPA multi-block hyperparameters with "Temporal mask scale 1" (P404, App. C Table 5). V-JEPA's own ablation found multi-block better than causal multi-block (P201, Table 4). So the paper's "contiguous block of future patches" description does not match V-JEPA 1/2 pretraining as documented. The direction sensitivity they measure arises **without** a future-only/causal masking objective. That weakens the paper's mechanistic attribution and directly concerns H2's premise. What is supported is "latent-space masked prediction (non-causal, full-clip) yields high reversal sensitivity". The claim that "future prediction is the cause" is not supported.
- Other confounds the authors admit (App. A): input resolution and clip length are inherited from each release. Their findings are "evidence consistent with the pretraining objective contributing", not "an absolute causal effect".

## Critical analysis

- Strengths: probe-free reversal cosine, replicated on a second dataset (within ~0.10). Data-scale control. Honest limitations.
- INFERENCE: cos_rev is per-token (spatiotemporal token pairs), not a pooled clip embedding. Per-token cosine is much more sensitive than a GAP/pooled embedding, because pooling over time averages out order. Our measured 0.959 is a hand-weighted mean over windows, i.e., pooled, and is not comparable to their 0.4–0.85 values. A like-for-like comparison would need per-window, per-token cosine on SignRep.
- INFERENCE: the mechanistic explanation (future-block masking) conflicts with V-JEPA's documented masking (see Mechanism). Treat "latent prediction → arrow of time" as a correlation across five public checkpoints with many uncontrolled differences (data, resolution, clip length, distillation).
- Evaluation is classification-centric. There is no retrieval/kNN reversal metric beyond a figure-level kNN on 600 videos.

## Overlap with H1/H2

- H2 (continued SignRep pretraining with cross-window latent prediction of a frozen teacher's future-window embedding, plus a self-distillation anchor).
  - What this paper already establishes: a measurement protocol for arrow-of-time sensitivity of frozen encoders (per-token cos_rev, antonym flip rate, DSCS), and the observation that latent-prediction encoders are far more reversal-sensitive than contrastive or pixel-MAE encoders on general video.
  - What it does not do: train anything, test the sign domain, test future-window (cross-clip) prediction, test retrieval/kNN under reversal, or isolate objective from recipe.
  - Implication: H2's motivating story ("predict the future, therefore become direction-aware") is already in the literature as an *explanation*, but the evidence actually comes from non-causal masked latent prediction. H2's delta must be shown against a **bidirectional/non-causal** latent-prediction control at equal compute. Otherwise, the "future" part is unmotivated.
- H1: indirectly relevant. It supplies the reversal metric family and the observation that pooled/contrastive features are stable but not useful (VideoPrism: high cos, low accuracy). Our weighted mean is in the same regime. No method overlap.
- Separating experiment for H2: the same continued-pretraining budget from the SignRep checkpoint with three arms: (a) future-window latent prediction (H2), (b) symmetric masked-window latent prediction (past+future context, V-JEPA-style non-causal), (c) continued SignRep objective only (steps-matched control, required by rule 20). Report per-token and pooled reversal cosine plus ASL Citizen leave-signer-out R@1. H2's "future" claim survives only if (a) > (b) on direction sensitivity at no R@1 loss.

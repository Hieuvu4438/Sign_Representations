## Evaluation (validation only)

- Extract val windows at stride 4 with the arm's encoder (`features`); pooling weights = **original model's** activity outputs at the same window starts (cached, EXT001), so readout is identical across arms.
- Leave-one-signer-out inside val: for each of the 6 val signers, gallery = the other 5 signers' clips (labels = gloss), class score = max cosine.
- Primary: R@1 (instance). Secondary: MRR, DCG, R@5, bins2 readout, chamfer rerank on the fixed 2000-query subset, per-ASL-LEX-movement-type R@1, clip reversal cosine on 500 clips of the subset (reverse extraction for M0/C0 only, if budget allows).
- Uncertainty: paired per-query differences, signer-cluster bootstrap (6 clusters; coarse) + per-signer signs.

## Decision rules (fixed now)

- PASS (→ G4 candidate, then confirmatory protocol): M0 − C0 ≥ +1.0 R@1 and M0 − A0 ≥ +1.0, ≥ 5/6 signers positive vs C0, and gain on {non-repeated, path-moving} signs larger than on {repeated or None movement}.
- REFUTE pilot: M0 − C0 ≤ 0.
- INCONCLUSIVE: in between → at most one revision (e.g. Δ or λ), declared before running.
- INVALID: C0 or A0 below B0 by > 2.0 R@1 (destructive continuation) → fix recipe first.

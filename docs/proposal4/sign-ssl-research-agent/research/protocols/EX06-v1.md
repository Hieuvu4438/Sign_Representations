# EX06-v1 — Generality of the readout effect to a second SSL encoder (SHuBERT)

## Status

LOCKED 2026-10-06 ~06:30 +07, before any SHuBERT retrieval number was computed.

## Setting

- Encoder: SHuBERT (ACL 2025) author checkpoint `checkpoint_836_400000.pt` (sha256 9087f476…), features extracted by `method/p4_signssl/shubert/extract.py` (fidelity to the author pipeline documented in `shubert/README.md`; bit-identical on fidelity clips). Pre-declared layer: **12 (final)**; per-frame features at native frame rate.
- Data: ASL Citizen **val** only (10,304 clips, 6 signers), leave-one-signer-out (gallery = the other 5 val signers; class-max cosine). Same protocol as the SignRep val-LOSO numbers (C001/EX02: SignRep paper readout 0.4950 R@1 at stride 4; 0.4925 at stride 2).
- Frames are SHuBERT's natural unit (contextualised over the whole clip), so "windows" = frames subsampled at stride 2 for set/sequence readouts.

## Readouts (fixed)

S0 mean of all frames (SHuBERT authors' ISLR pooling is a time average); S1 mean over the valid span (first..last frame with `frame_valid`); S2 bins2 over the valid span (two halves, L2-normalised, concatenated); S3 chamfer@20 over valid-span frames at stride 2 (≤ 48 kept by uniform subsampling), rerank of S1's top-20 classes.

## Decision

Report all; the generality question is answered positively if S2 − S0 > 0 and S3 − S0 > 0 with signer-cluster CI > 0 (6 clusters, coarse). The magnitude is descriptive (no δ), because the SHuBERT readout differs structurally (contextual frames, no activity head).

## Amendment EX06b (2026-10-06, written after the FFN-branch run and before any residual-stream number)

The author extraction returns the FFN-branch output of each block (`layer_results[i][-1]`), which is at floor for frozen kNN (S0 0.96%, ≤ 4.6% for any layer). EX06b re-extracts val with the block output / residual stream (`layer_results[i][0]`, `shubert/extract.py --hidden residual`) and applies the same four readouts, layer 12 pre-declared.

## Results (val leave-one-signer-out, 10,304 queries)

| Features | S0 mean all | S1 mean valid span | S2 bins2 | S3 chamfer@20 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FFN branch (author semantics) | 0.0096 | 0.0254 | 0.0246 | 0.0140 |
| Residual stream (EX06b) | 0.0418 | 0.1032 | **0.1385** | **0.1434** |

EX06b paired differences (signer clusters): S2 − S1 +3.53 [+2.20, +4.08] R@1, S3 − S1 +4.03 [+2.44, +5.05], 5/6 signers; vs S0: +9.67 / +10.16. Per-layer mean-all R@1 (descriptive) peaks at layers 7–8 (0.060).
Verdict: readout effect **generalises to SHuBERT** in direction (ordered/set readouts > span mean), at a low absolute level of frozen kNN accuracy.

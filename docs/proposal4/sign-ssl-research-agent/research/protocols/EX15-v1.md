# EX15-v1 — Screening: JEPA vs MAE and target priors on V-JEPA 2.1 ViT-B (H014)

## Status

LOCKED 2026-10-07 ~17:00 +07, before any EX15 training run. Screening/development protocol on ASL Citizen **val only**; no test split is used. Any confirmatory claim needs a later protocol.

## Fixed recipe (all arms)

- Init: official V-JEPA 2.1 ViT-B distilled from ViT-G (`ema_encoder` weights; sha256 in `method/jepa_targets/vendor/PROVENANCE.md`); predictor body (12 blocks, d = 384) from the same checkpoint; new output projection sized to the arm's dense targets (the released projection targets ViT-G features).
- Data: unlabelled ASL Citizen **train** clips (40,154), one random 16-frame window per clip draw, squash-resized to 224×224, ImageNet normalisation; no augmentation.
- Masks: official 2.1 ViT-B multi-block masks (8 blocks at 0.15 + 2 blocks at 0.7, temporal scale 1.0), both generators each step.
- Optimiser: AdamW lr 1e-4 (300-step warm-up, cosine to 1%), wd 0.04, grad-clip 1.0, bf16; batch 32; **4,000 steps**; seed 0; EMA encoder (momentum 0.99925) kept in every arm and evaluated.
- Losses: latent = L1 to layer-normed EMA tokens at masked tokens + 0.5 × context loss (2.1 recipe); pixel = MSE to per-patch normalised pixels at masked tokens (VideoMAE); HOG = MSE to MaskFeat-style HOG (scaled to unit per-dim magnitude); priors = smooth-L1 on robust-standardised (median/MAD, clip ±5) per-slice priors from a head on the online encoder's context tokens, weight 1.0.
- Code: `method/jepa_targets/jt/{vjepa,data,priors,targets,train,extract,evaluate}.py`.

## Evaluation (val only, `jt/evaluate.py`)

- Windows: 16 frames every 8 frames; window feature = mean of final encoder tokens.
- Primary: leave-one-signer-out retrieval inside val (6 signers), clip embedding = mean of L2-normalised windows; R@1. Secondary: two-halves readout; DCG/MRR.
- Body-part probes: window-level ridge (α = 100) from standardised features to window-averaged priors, signer-disjoint 2-fold; R² for handshape, body, location/relations, non-manual, movement. Note: probing a family an arm was trained on is not independent evidence for that arm.
- Signer probe (logistic regression on clip embeddings, gloss-disjoint split); embedding geometry (mean cosine, effective rank) as collapse check.

## Decision rules (fixed now)

- "X beats Y": R@1 difference ≥ +1.0 with signer-cluster bootstrap 95% CI > 0 (only 6 clusters; per-signer differences reported).
- Q1 answered "JEPA better" if J beats M **and** J+P ≥ M+P; "MAE better" if the reverse; otherwise "no clear difference at this budget".
- A target family is "useful" if J+X beats J. The best 1–2 configurations are then repeated with seeds 1, 2 before any statement in the paper; single-seed results are reported as screening.
- Collapse guard: an arm whose val clip embeddings have mean cosine > 0.99 or effective rank < 10 is reported as collapsed regardless of R@1.

## Amendment v1.1 (2026-10-07 ~17:45, before any EX15 training arm was evaluated)

Seen at amendment time: only the two references (A0 frozen V-JEPA: LOSO R@1 0.20%, mean cosine 0.98; SignRep plain-mean readout 30.4%). Change: body-part probe ridge α is chosen per model and sub-group by inner leave-one-signer-out CV inside the training fold from {1e2, 1e3, 1e4, 1e5} (fixed α = 100 gave negative R² for A0 at every group while α = 1e4 gave positive R², and the best α differed between models). Retrieval readout unchanged (feature centering was checked on the references and did not help A0: 0.24%; it lowered SignRep: 27.9%). Note for interpretation: the released SignRep reference uses the plain-mean readout here (30.4%); with its own activity weighting at stride 4 it reached 49.5% LOSO (EX13), so this protocol's readout handicaps models with rest segments equally.

## Amendment v1.2 (2026-10-07 ~18:20, after seeing A0 and arm M only; before any JEPA arm result)

Observed: the locked plain-mean readout is at chance for A0 (0.20%) and M (0.16%) — clip embeddings are dominated by a shared appearance direction (mean cosine 0.98). Label-free PCA whitening (fit on the evaluated split's unlabelled windows, k = 512) recovers signal: A0 6.6%, M 4.1%, SignRep 37.5% (k = 64/256 also checked: lower). Change: **primary metric becomes LOSO R@1 with the whitened-512 mean readout** (also whitened two-halves); the locked plain readouts are still reported. The decision rules apply to the whitened readout and the paired bootstrap uses it. Disclosure: the switch was made after seeing M < A0 under whitening, which goes against the MAE arm only through information already seen; no JEPA-arm number was available.

## Amendment v1.3 (2026-10-08, user question "is your MAE trained like SignRep?"; before MP/JP/S results)

Re-read of SignRep §4–5 / §7.2 (arXiv 2503.08529v1, HTML grep): SignRep keeps MAE-style input masking (Hiera-B initialised from Kinetics MAE) but **replaces the pixel-reconstruction decoder** by a lightweight sign decoder on the mean of the encoder tokens, trained with L1 reconstruction of sign priors (confidence > 50%), plus variance/covariance feature regularisation and an adversarial style-agnostic loss; pretraining on YouTube-SL-25. Therefore arm M is plain MAE (not SignRep) and M+P is pixel + priors (also not SignRep). Added arm **S (SignRep-style)**: masked input → prior head on context tokens (POSE, smooth-L1) + variance/covariance regularisation (weight 1.0 on pooled context features; `--vicreg`), no pixel loss; adversarial style loss omitted (declared deviation); backbone remains V-JEPA 2.1 ViT-B (not Hiera-B) so that only the objective differs. Paradigm comparison for the user's question: J+P (JEPA + priors) vs S (SignRep-style priors-only). Decision rule as before (≥ +1.0, CI > 0). Runner `runtime/run_ex15b.sh` (MP → JP → S → Stage 2). `jt/train.py` changed only by adding the default-off `--vicreg` option.

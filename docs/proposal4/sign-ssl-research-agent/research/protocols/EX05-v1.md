## Fixed readouts (no tuning after lock)

1. R0 — SignRep paper readout: activity-weighted mean of window `features` over the whole clip, γ = clip(max(P_LH,P_RH),0,1).
2. R1 — bins2: active span (γ ≥ 0.5; whole clip if none), 2 equal halves by window index, activity-weighted mean per half, L2-normalised halves concatenated (/√2).
3. R2 — chamfer@20: rerank the top-20 classes of the active-span weighted mean by symmetric chamfer similarity of L2-normalised active-span windows (≤ 48 windows), class score = max over that class's gallery clips.
All with windows of 16 native frames, stride 2, gallery = official train split, class-max cosine (R0, R1).

## Metrics and statistics

Primary: R@1 difference R1 − R0 and R2 − R0 on ASL Citizen test. Secondary: MRR, DCG, R@5; WLASL test. Paired signer-cluster bootstrap (11 test signers, 10,000 resamples) for ASL Citizen; for WLASL (no signer IDs) paired bootstrap over classes (2,000 classes). Two primary comparisons → Holm correction at α = 0.05.

## Decision rules

- SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE if R1 − R0 ≥ +2.0 R@1 with Holm-adjusted CI > 0 on ASL Citizen test (δ = 2.0 chosen as ≈ 1/3 of the val effect, i.e. a clearly practical gain), and the sign of the effect is the same on WLASL test.
- REFUTED if R1 − R0 ≤ 0 on ASL Citizen test.
- Otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. Same rules applied to R2 (secondary, as it is not single-vector).

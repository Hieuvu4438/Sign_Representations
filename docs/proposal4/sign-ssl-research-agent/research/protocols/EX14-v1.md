## Data and training

- ASL Citizen: models trained on unlabelled ASL Citizen train windows (H011 dev model reused; H012b clusters `restpose_clusters_train.npy`).
- WLASL2000, MS-ASL: fresh models on each dataset's own unlabelled train windows; pseudo-signer clusters recomputed on that train set with the same rule (rest-segment descriptor, K-means, K ∈ {20, 40, 60, 80, 120} by silhouette). No labels or signer metadata used.
- Measured before lock: rest segments are missing for 59.0% (WLASL) and 66.8% (MS-ASL) of train clips vs 5.5% (ASL Citizen); H12b on those datasets is a degraded variant (fallback first+last windows) and is reported as such.
- Seeds: primary arm 3 seeds on ASL Citizen (0, 1, 2), 1 seed on WLASL/MS-ASL (disclosed).

## Statistics and decision

- Primary: ASL Citizen test, primary arm − H8 (3-seed mean; per seed signer-cluster bootstrap 10,000 and exact sign-flip over 11 test signers).
  - SUPPORTED_WITHIN_SCOPE: 3-seed mean ≥ +1.0 R@1, every seed's 95% CI > 0.
  - REFUTED: 3-seed mean ≤ 0.
  - otherwise INCONCLUSIVE.
- Secondary (Holm over the family {primary arm − M2D2 on ASL Citizen, primary arm − H8 on WLASL, primary arm − H8 on MS-ASL, other included arms − H8 on ASL Citizen}).
- Test-use disclosure: all three test splits were evaluated before (EX05, EX07, EX12, EX12b) for other readouts/models; none of their labels was used to develop or select any EX14 arm. This is a re-used test, not a fresh holdout; claims are scoped accordingly.

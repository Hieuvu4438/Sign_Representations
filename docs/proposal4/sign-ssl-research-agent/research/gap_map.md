## Critique of the gaps (reviewer pass, same agent, knew all EXPL numbers above)

- GAP1 could be **span selection**, not order: active-span mean already reaches 0.479 vs full-clip mean 0.322; the order-specific gain is bins2 vs active-span weighted mean (+4.2), which must be re-measured with the real train gallery.
- Bin-shuffle collapse shows bins differ, not that order is *semantically* informative; the stronger order evidence is reversal-invariance (cos 0.959) together with the bins gain.
- Reversed signs are often non-signs, so reversal-invariance is "harmless" for closed-vocabulary retrieval unless the vocabulary contains direction-contrasting pairs; its value is as a mechanism diagnostic, and as a risk for open vocabularies (e.g. GIVE/RECEIVE-type direction contrasts — needs a validated list, not invented by the agent).
- GAP2 lift values are not comparable across parameters with different cardinalities without a control representation; needs a pose-motion control.
- All EXPL numbers come from a 5-signer gallery; the official train gallery (35 signers) may shrink or enlarge the differences.

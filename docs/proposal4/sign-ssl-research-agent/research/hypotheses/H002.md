# H002 — Cross-window latent prediction (XWP) makes averaged sign embeddings keep movement

## Identity

| Trường | Giá trị |
| --- | --- |
| Status | INCONCLUSIVE→not pursued (D003): M0−C0 = +0.07 R@1 on val LOSO; gain over B0 explained by continuation |
| Version / date | v1, 2026-10-05 |
| Gap IDs / source evidence | GAP1, GAP2 (`gap_map.md`); EXPL numbers in `cycles/C001.md` and below |
| Main question | Does a label-free objective that makes each 16-frame window predict the frozen teacher embedding of its past/future neighbour window make the **standard averaged** clip embedding retain movement information that averaging currently destroys? |
| Allowed supervision | None beyond SignRep's own pretraining (its pose teacher is inherited through the frozen teacher). ASL Citizen **train videos without labels** as continuation data. Labels only for evaluation. |
| Primary scope | ASL Citizen frozen dictionary retrieval, RGB inference, same readout (activity-weighted mean, weights from the original model) for every arm |

## Lập luận có thể kiểm tra

- Observation (EXPL, val leave-one-signer-out, n=2000 queries): order-free chamfer matching of window sets beats the averaged embedding by +6.5 R@1 (0.567 vs 0.502); adding a temporal-position penalty adds nothing (0.5705); the gain is largest for non-repeated path movements (+8.6) and smallest for repeated or no-movement signs (+4.1 / +3.5). The averaged clip embedding is nearly time-reversal invariant (cos 0.959). Confusions share handshape/location far above chance, movement close to chance.
- Mechanism (assumed): window embeddings mostly encode the momentary configuration (handshape/location); movement shows up as *change across windows*, which the mean blurs. If every window must also predict the teacher embedding of the window ±Δ frames away, its embedding must encode where the articulators come from/go to, so the mean of windows keeps transition information.
- Minimal change: continuation loss = anchor (masked student → frozen teacher, same window) + λ·prediction of teacher embedding at offset Δ ∈ {−8, +8} through a small Δ-conditioned predictor (discarded at inference).
- Predictions: (P1) M0 > C0 (Δ=0, same predictor/compute) and > A0 (anchor only) on averaged-readout R@1; (P2) the gain concentrates on non-repeated path-movement signs; (P3) clip embeddings become more sensitive to time reversal than C0; (P4) the chamfer − mean gap shrinks.
- Strongest alternative explanation: any continuation on in-domain ASL Citizen video (domain adaptation) or masked-to-full self-distillation improves retrieval regardless of the temporal target → controlled by C0/A0 which share data, masking, steps and predictor.
- Refutation: M0 − C0 < +1.0 R@1 (pilot) or gain not larger on path-movement signs, or reversal sensitivity unchanged.
- Not concludable from this test: linguistic "understanding" of movement; generality beyond ASL Citizen; benefit for fine-tuned recognition.

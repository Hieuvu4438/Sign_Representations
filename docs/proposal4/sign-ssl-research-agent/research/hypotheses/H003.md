## What was tested (EX03-v1, LOCKED before results)

Geometry-only teacher states from local COCO-WholeBody 2-D keypoints for all 40,154 ASL Citizen train videos (labels never read); LH/RH/FACE/BODY states (43/43/102/17 dims), calibrated on 2 held-out signers; 5 signer folds over the other 33 signers; identical G0/G1/Gsh transformers (spec §9.1 sizes), 4000 steps each, best-of-16 on forecaster_val; common-support out-of-fold evaluation on ≈ 3.5 M (example × edge × horizon) rows; signer-cluster bootstrap.

## Result (REPRODUCED_RESULT, `method/p4_signssl/runtime/cip/gates.json`, log `runtime/logs/EX03.log`)

- **0 / 36 edge-horizon gates pass.** Best relative gains: BODY→RH@4 +6.2% (CI of Δ includes 0, and G1 worse than shuffled-peer Gsh), FACE→RH ≤ +0.8%, LH→RH ≤ +0.4%. 25/36 edges have **negative** relative gain (peer history hurts on held-out signers).
- Ridge-regression check (`scripts/cip_ridge_check.py`, train folds 1–4 → fold 0): LH→RH and RH→LH at δ = 2/4/8 all show G1 worse than G0 (−0.7% … −2.5% MSE; median |error| also worse) → the null is not an artefact of the neural forecasters.
- All forecasters beat persistence clearly (e.g. LH→RH@4 G0 1.46 vs persistence 2.66), so the forecasting task is learnable; the **cross-articulator increment** is what is absent.

## Interpretation and limits

- Per spec §10.5 the CIP target is effectively zero in this setting → the student pilot (C1 vs B4/B6) was **not** run; λ_aux would be 0 and C1 would reduce to the dense-state baseline.
- INFERENCE: in isolated signs, each hand's own 8-frame history already carries the predictable part of its future (including symmetric/alternating two-handed signs, where the peer's information is redundant with own phase). Remaining unpredictability is signer-specific or detector noise, which does not transfer across signers.
- Not concluded: CIP may behave differently with (a) continuous signing (YouTube-SL-25: more coarticulation and longer context), (b) MediaPipe 3-D hand landmarks + DINO appearance states, (c) 30 Hz resampling. These are the spec's canonical conditions and were unavailable locally (no YouTube-SL-25 videos on disk; download not attempted). The negative result is scoped to: ASL Citizen isolated signs, 2-D COCO-WholeBody geometry, native frame rate.

## Continuous-signing check (EX08-v1, LOCKED, 2026-10-06)

8,000 random How2Sign train sentence clips (text never read), same 2-D COCO-WholeBody geometry states, groups = YouTube video id, 5 group folds, identical forecasters/gates. Result: **0/36 gates**; best relative gain +0.1% (RH→LH@8), 33/36 edges negative; every forecaster beats persistence. The "isolated signs" limitation is removed; the remaining untested conditions are the 3-D MediaPipe hand teacher and the DINO appearance block (`runtime/cip_how2sign/gates.json`, log `runtime/logs/EX08.log`).

## Appearance + 3-D hand teacher (EX09-v1, LOCKED, 2026-10-06)

1,200 How2Sign clips (203k frames) through the SHuBERT author preprocessing; states = [DINOv2 (author hand/face fine-tuned) appearance PCA-128 | MediaPipe 3-D hand / pose geometry], block-weighted MSE (proposal5 §11.1). Appearance coverage LH 0.91, RH 0.92, FACE 1.00. Result: **0/36 gates, all 36 relative gains negative** (best −1.4%). Caveat: at δ = 2 persistence sometimes beats the learned G0 (small training set), but G1 and G0 share capacity and budget and G1 never improves on G0. Remaining untested: DINOv3 generic teacher, YouTube-SL-25-scale data (`runtime/cip_teacher_states/gates.json`, logs `EX09_extract.log`, `EX09.log`).

## Decision

D004: do not implement the CIP student pilot on this data. Re-open only if a continuous-signing source with a stronger hand teacher becomes available; a first check there would be this same gate test (cost ≈ 1.5 GPU-h after state extraction).

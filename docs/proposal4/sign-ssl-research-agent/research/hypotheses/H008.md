## Mechanism (testable)

The frozen windows hold more retrievable information than their mean (C012, C017); window-set matching exposes part of it (phase coverage + within-window direction, see reversed-query diagnostic). A student trained to reproduce the *ranking* of window-set similarities among unlabelled neighbours should compress that information into one vector. Controls already run: same student with a mean-similarity teacher (CM, 0.588) and instance discrimination (CI, 0.647).

## Development budget (declared before any dev run, 2026-10-06)

Selection on ASL Citizen val R@1 (official gallery), 1 seed, at most 3 configurations, everything else as EX04-v1 (batch 64, K = 64 wmean candidates + 8 in-batch, τ_student = τ_teacher):

| Config | Teacher | Steps | τ |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

The chosen configuration is then frozen in EX12 (confirmatory: train on each dataset's unlabelled train split; test once on ASL Citizen, WLASL2000, MS-ASL; compare with R0 / R1 / R2 on identical windows).

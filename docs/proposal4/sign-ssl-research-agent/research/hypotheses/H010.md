## Predictions written before the diagnostic numbers (2026-10-07)

- P1: T1 (temporal-difference chamfer) and/or T2 (clip-centred) lower the same-signer rate of top-1 neighbours relative to T0 and raise the same-gloss-different-signer rate.
- P2: a teacher that raises same-gloss-different-signer top-1 precision by ≥ 5 points absolute over T0 without losing > 1 point of val readout R@1 is worth distilling (→ EX14 protocol).
- Falsifier: no teacher variant changes the signer/gloss composition of neighbours by ≥ 5 points → the "signer-dominated teacher" explanation of the label-free gap is not actionable with frozen window features; pivot (e.g. part/ROI-pooled features, encoder-level change).

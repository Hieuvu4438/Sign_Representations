| Hypothesis | The 7.6 M-parameter student fits per-anchor teacher distributions on the 38k training anchors but generalises them poorly. Regularising the student input (window dropout) and enlarging the unlabelled anchor pool (other ASL datasets' TRAIN windows) shrink the held-out KL and raise val R@1 toward the teacher |
| Supervision | none (other datasets' train windows are unlabelled; no gloss, no signer IDs) |
| Semantic-preservation argument for window dropout | SignRep windows are 16 frames at stride 2, so adjacent windows share 14 of 16 frames; dropping each window with p = 0.25 (keeping ≥ 1) removes no frame from the clip with high probability for spans ≥ 8 windows and does not reorder time (positions are kept). It is applied to the student only; the teacher sees all windows. Not a time-reversal, flip or crop. Short clips (< 8 windows) can lose coverage; reported as a limitation |
| Data note | Extra pools: `wlasl_train_s2` (14,296), `msasl_train_s2` (12,711) TRAIN clips only. WLASL/MS-ASL test splits are untouched by this; MS-ASL train overlaps YouTube-ASL sources (already disclosed). The ASL Citizen retrieval gallery stays ASL Citizen train only |

## Predictions and decision (val, compared with H011 dev 0.6919, paired signer-cluster bootstrap)

- P1: at least one of A1/A2 reduces the held-out/train KL ratio (H011: 5.5×) by ≥ 30%.
- P2: best arm − H011 dev ≥ +1.5 val R@1, CI > 0.
- P1 without P2 → the gap is real but not what limits retrieval; record and stop.
- Neither → the gap explanation is wrong; record.
- P2 met → a new confirmatory protocol (EX15) is needed; EX14 is not modified.

## Predictions (declared before running)

- P1 (signal exists): rest-pose descriptors cluster ASL Citizen train clips by signer with NMI ≥ 0.8 vs true signer IDs at K = the number that maximises silhouette on unlabelled data (K grid 20–120; true K reported only for reference).
- P2 (actionable): among each anchor's 64 wmean candidates, reranking by T4 and excluding candidates in the anchor's pseudo-signer cluster raises top-1 same-gloss-different-signer rate by ≥ 5 points over T4 without exclusion (0.416 in H010).
- P3 (content leakage control): rest-pose descriptors alone retrieve the correct gloss on val far less than the active-span descriptors (R@1 < 10%); otherwise they are not "content-free" and exclusion could remove true positives for content reasons.
- Falsifier: NMI < 0.6 or P2 gain < 2 points → pseudo-signer mining is not actionable with frozen SignRep windows; record and stop.

## Go rule

P1 and P2 met → H012 student dev on val (same aggregator/budget as H008 D2, teacher T0 and T4 with cross-cluster candidate lists; 2 arms), compared to H008 D2 and H011 under a declared ≥ +1.5 R@1, signer-cluster CI > 0 rule.

# H006 — Chamfer-mined pseudo-positives to distil set matching into the encoder

| Trường | Giá trị |
| --- | --- |
| Status | REJECTED at diagnostic stage (2026-10-06), no training run |
| Idea | Label-free: for each unlabelled ASL Citizen train clip, mine its best chamfer neighbour (top-50 wmean candidates, chamfer rerank) as a positive; continue SignRep so averaged embeddings of mined pairs agree (NNCLR-like with a stronger set-matching teacher) |
| Closest prior art | NNCLR (nearest-neighbour positives), DnS (set-similarity distillation), cluster pseudo-labels |

## Diagnostic (labels/signers used only to score mining quality; `scripts/explore_mining.py`, 4,000 train anchors)

| Miner | same gloss | same signer | same gloss & different signer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wmean top-1 | 0.391 | 0.434 | 0.327 |
| chamfer top-1 | 0.438 | 0.487 | 0.368 |
| chamfer, top 10% most confident | 0.355 | 0.823 | — |

Mining confidence tracks **signer identity**, not sign identity: the most confident pairs are 82% same-signer. Training on them would reinforce the signer component that the same-signer distractor test (WLASL) and whitening diagnostic already exposed.

## Decision

Reject without training (cost-saving). A version with signer-aware filtering needs signer metadata (a different supervision setting) or a label-free signer proxy that does not rely on face re-identification; not pursued (project rules).

## Why the scores sit near SignRep (measured)

Warm-start + EMA teacher initialised at SignRep (momentum 0.996→0.9999) means every latent target is SignRep's own feature; only the 2-D geometry term (γ = 0.1) brings outside information. Budget 3000 × 8 clips (≈ 0.6 epoch of ASL Citizen train); backbone weights changed by 1.7% (‖ΔW‖/‖W‖), while window embeddings rotated (mean cosine to the original 0.75–0.77) with neighbourhood structure largely preserved.

## Interpretation

- In this setting, ASC-JEPA's local terms mainly reduce the degradation caused by JEPA continuation; the core contributor is ROI-pooled state distillation, not the change term or the routing gate.
- The warm-start branch cannot test the proposal's main claim (better representations when pretraining from a generic init at scale). A scaled-down controlled chain (Hiera MAE-K400 init, ≈ 80 h local video, B1 vs B4, 10–15 GPU-h per arm) is the cheapest decisive test; given that change+routing add ≤ 0.4 points here, its expected value is low.
